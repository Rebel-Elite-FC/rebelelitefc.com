Our story

Southern Utah's rebel club.

Started so St. George girls didn't have to drive to Las Vegas or Salt Lake for elite soccer. Now a two-time national champion.

Why we exist

For years the best young players in Washington County had two choices: settle for recreational soccer, or spend weekends on I-15 chasing clubs in Las Vegas and the Wasatch Front. Ignite FC was founded around a St. George kitchen table in May 2018 to give local kids a third option — real coaching, real competition, and a real club family, at home.

Rebel Elite grew up alongside it as a girls' program and joined the Ignite FC family in March 2023. Within four months a Rebel team had won a national championship. We stayed close-knit on purpose: our coaches know every player's name, strengths, and goals, and we measure success by how much each girl improves — and by who she becomes along the way.

What we stand for

Character first

Effort, respect, and accountability are coached as deliberately as a first touch.

Development over trophies

We play to win, but we never trade a player's long-term growth for a short-term result.

Club family

Parents, siblings, and sponsors are part of the team. Nobody travels to Nationals alone.

Southern Utah proud

We represent St. George everywhere we go — and we go far.

College pathway

From Rebel Elite to the next level.

Our U15–U19 program is built around what college coaches actually look for: technical quality, game intelligence, athleticism, and character. Players get help building highlight film, a recruiting profile, a target-school list, and introductions at showcase events — plus honest guidance from coaches who have played at the college level.

Part of Ignite FC

One club, two crests.

Rebel Elite is the girls' side of Ignite FC — Southern Utah's elite soccer experience. Boys play as Ignite in orange; girls play as Rebel Elite in pink and black. Same board, same registrar, same standards, and the same home at the Sullivan and Little Valley fields. Ignite FC is a member of the Utah Youth Soccer Association and, since 2026, US Club Soccer, and hosts the Zion Champions Cup every fall.
