Questions

Frequently asked

Which team will my daughter be placed on?

Placement is by age group first, then by tryout evaluation. Black is typically the top team in an age group, followed by White and Pink. Players may be invited to play up when it is right for their development.

Can teams play across age groups?

Under the new school-year alignment, teams are not forced to split, but most players will play in the older of two possible groups. Play-down exceptions are reviewed case by case and granted for one year only.

Do you have boys' teams?

Boys play for Ignite FC, our parent club, under the same coaching philosophy. Visit ignitefc.com.

How many tournaments do teams play?

Each team chooses and funds its own tournaments. Younger teams typically play 2–4 a year; older teams 5–8, including State Cup, Presidents Cup, and college showcases like our own Zion Champions Cup.

Ready to play for Rebel Elite?

Girls U8–U19 in St. George. Tryouts every June — evaluations year-round.
