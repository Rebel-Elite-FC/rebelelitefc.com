How it works

Three steps to a Rebel jersey.

  1. Register online. Registration runs through Ollie Sports and takes about three minutes. It opens each spring; UYSA rules keep every Utah club's tryouts in the same early-June window.
  2. Come to tryouts. Bring water, a ball, cleats, and shin guards. Sessions run by age group at Sullivan Soccer Complex — arrive 15–20 minutes early for check-in.
  3. Get your offer. Coaches contact families within a few days of the final session with team placement and next steps.

Questions

Frequently asked

When are Rebel Elite tryouts?

Every club in Utah holds tryouts in the same window set by UYSA — the first week of June. Registration opens in the spring on Ollie Sports, and the schedule by age group is posted here and on ignitefc.com. Players can be evaluated year-round if a roster has room.

Where are tryouts held?

At Sullivan Soccer Complex in St. George. Arrive 15–20 minutes before your first session for check-in.

Who can try out?

Girls U8 through U19. No prior club experience is required for our younger age groups. U8 players may play up with U9.

What should my player bring?

Water, a ball, cleats, and shin guards. Attend every session you can — more time in front of the coaches is better, but one night is welcome.

What does it cost to play?

The 2026-27 club fee is $195 per player ($163 for high-school players who play only one of the two seasons) and covers coaching, administration, and fields, with $40 of it going to a team equipment fund. UYSA registration ($220–$280) and the adidas Rebel kit (about $197 youth / $214 adult, replaced every two years) are paid separately, and teams fund their own tournaments.

Which age group is my daughter in?

Starting with the 2026-27 season, UYSA uses a school-year alignment with an August 1 cutoff: players born January–July are in one age group and players born August–December in the next younger group. See the chart on our Teams page.

Where do teams train and play?

Practices are usually two nights a week at Sullivan Soccer Complex and Little Valley Soccer Complex. League games run in a fall season and a spring season (8–15 games each), usually one night a week, mostly at the Shivwits fields (past Ivins).

We're from out of town. Can we still try out?

Yes. Email us your player's name and birthdate and we'll connect you with the right coach or arrange another option.
