Rebel Elite Director & Coach

Chris BrownMBA, PhD · Ignite FC Board

Chris has coached club soccer in Southern Utah for more than a decade. He founded the original Rebel Elite teams, brought them into the Ignite FC family in 2023, and has coached Rebel sides to Presidents Cup state titles, a Far West Regional championship, and the national finals.

Off the field he is an assistant professor of business and entrepreneurship at Utah Tech University and the founder of several companies. He brings the same mindset to coaching: clear standards, individual development plans, and a culture where players own their growth.

Chris and his wife have six kids — most of whom have worn a Rebel jersey.

Coach & Goalkeeper Training

Adelle BrownFormer college goalkeeper

Adelle was ranked #1 in the nation in NJCAA saves and earned first-team all-conference honors at Iowa Lakes Community College before playing at Dixie State University (now Utah Tech), where she earned RMAC honorable mention and helped the team reach the NCAA Division II Elite Eight as region champions.

She leads Rebel Elite's goalkeeper training — footwork, distribution, shot-stopping, and the mentality the position demands.
