Why Rebel Elite

Hometown club. National standard.

Rebel Elite started so St. George girls didn't have to drive to Las Vegas or Salt Lake for elite soccer. Today our teams win state titles every January and have brought two national championships home to Southern Utah — with coaches who know every player by name and push each one to get better every week.

  • 24 girls' teams, U8 through U19 — a team for every level
  • Licensed, background-checked coaches at every session
  • Goalkeeper-specific training included
  • State Cup, Presidents Cup & showcase tournaments
  • Club fee of $195 — tournaments chosen by each team

Proven on the national stage

Two national titles. Three years.

Rebel Elite Black won the Presidents Cup National Championship at U14 in 2023 and again at U18 in 2025, with a Far West Regional title and two more national-final appearances in between. In January 2026 three Rebel teams won state championships in the same weekend.

  1. Jan 2026Rebel Elite 2012, 2010 and 2007 — Presidents Cup State Champions (U14, U16, U19)
  2. Jul 2025Rebel Elite Black U18 — Presidents Cup National Champions
  3. Jun 2025Rebel Elite Black U16 — State Cup Regional Champions
  4. Jan 2025Rebel Elite Black U13 — Presidents Cup State Champions
